# Go-to-Market: Aperta

Status: DRAFT v1, 2026-10-19 | Author: sales-agent | Scope: solves the two-sided cold start for a directory-only product (contact via Call/WhatsApp/Email, no booking or payments).

Note: PRD section 3.4 puts launch and GTM out of the demo scope. This doc is directional planning for post-demo (Phase 1+). Market facts below come from memory and are marked **(unverified)**; no live research was done.

## 0. Assumptions (no questions asked)

- Solo founder or 2-3 person team, near-zero budget (under Rs 50k/month), founder can do outreach personally.
- Phase 1 (supply: auth, profile editor, uploads, backend, hosting) ships before launch. The demo alone cannot onboard anyone.
- Founder has no pre-existing photographer network; has a base in or access to one metro.
- Revenue is not a goal for the first 6 months. Goal is proof of value: "Aperta sent me real enquiries".
- Directory-only stays until enquiry volume justifies more (PRD Phases 3-5).

## 1. Which side first, and the wedge

**Win supply first, but only a thin, curated slice, then pull demand to it.** Reasons:

1. Clients judge a directory in one visit. Empty or thin categories kill trust permanently; a photographer who joins early and gets no leads is only mildly annoyed, and the listing was free.
2. Photographers are findable and reachable by name (Instagram, WhatsApp). Clients are anonymous and diffuse. Supply is a sales problem you control; demand is a marketing problem you do not.
3. Photographers are also a demand channel: each one shares their profile link with their own clients and followers. Supply seeds demand.
4. Photographers have a real reason to join without demand: a free, clean, shareable portfolio-plus-packages link beats a Linktree/Instagram bio. This is the **single-player mode** that breaks the chicken-and-egg problem.

### Recommended wedge: Weddings, one city, one segment

- **Category: Weddings.** Highest ticket (typically Rs 50k to several lakh per package **(unverified)**), highest planning effort, and clients actively search and compare. Also where WedMeGood-style portals prove willingness to pay for leads **(unverified)**. Other three categories ride along passively (photographers serve several) but are not marketed at launch.
- **City: pick ONE of Jaipur or Udaipur-adjacent, otherwise Jaipur.** Rationale: Jaipur is a large wedding-spend market with a strong local vendor ecosystem and destination weddings **(unverified)**; mid-tier photographers are under-served by big portals compared with Delhi/Mumbai where competition and portal saturation are highest. Alternatives with the same logic: Lucknow, Chandigarh, Indore. If the founder lives elsewhere, use that city; founder proximity beats market-size analysis. The PRD seed already includes Jaipur (deep link examples).
- **Segment: mid-tier (Rs 40k to 1.5 lakh packages), 2-8 years experience, Instagram-active, under-served by the portals' sales teams.** Not the celebrity tier (do not need you), not the beginner tier (low spend, high churn, low client trust).
- **Target density: 40 photographers + 15 planners/decorators/venues in the wedding city.** Enough that a client browsing wedding photographers in the city sees 15-20 credible options with filters that actually narrow.

### Timing and seasonality (all **unverified**)

- Peak Indian wedding months are roughly Nov-Feb, with a secondary season Apr-Jun; Jul-Oct is lean (Chaturmas/monsoon) **(unverified)**. Auspicious dates shift each year.
- Today is 2026-10-19: the Nov-Feb season is imminent. Photographers are busiest and hardest to onboard Nov-Feb, but clients are most active Sep-Jan (booking 3-9 months ahead). Practical read: onboard supply NOW (before they are buried), launch demand pushes for the **Feb-Jun 2027 and Oct-Dec 2027 date booking window**, and use the busy season to collect real enquiry data.
- Realistic plan: run the 90-day plan below as Nov 2026 to Jan 2027 for supply + soft launch, accept that founding photographers will be shooting, and make onboarding concierge (we do the work) so it costs them 10 minutes.

### Why not other wedges

- Corporate events: buyers are HR/admin, reached via LinkedIn and referrals, slow cycles, harder to scale cheaply. Phase 2 channel.
- Birthdays/parties: low ticket, impulse, hard to rank in search. Free upside only.
- Portraits/fashion: strongest for Instagram, but buyers are price-sensitive and photographers are many. Later.
- Multi-city at launch: dilutes density. Do not.

## 3. Photographer acquisition playbook

### Where to find them

1. Instagram: hashtags `#jaipurweddingphotographer`, `#jaipurwedding`, location tags (City Palace, venues), followers of local planners and decorators. Target 2k-50k followers with recent posts and a phone number in bio.
2. Venue and planner tagged posts: the photographers they credit are already working and trusted.
3. Google Maps / Justdial "wedding photographer in <city>" listings **(Justdial unverified)**: reviews reveal active ones.
4. Facebook/WhatsApp groups for local photographers and vendors; local photography associations.
5. Referrals: every onboarded photographer asked for 3 names of peers (one "second shooter / editor" often has their own network).
6. Photo labs and album printers, equipment rental shops: the hub for local photographers.

### The offer

1. **Free Founding Listing, forever for the first 50.** No fee, no lock-in. "Founding Photographer" badge permanent.
2. **Concierge setup:** "Send me 12 best photos, your packages, and a bio in a WhatsApp voice note. I build the profile in 24 hours." Removes the biggest objection (effort). Doing this by hand for 40 people is the cheapest GTM spend there is.
3. **Lead guarantee (soft):** "If you get zero real enquiries in your first 60 days, we feature you on our front page and share your profile to our client list/Instagram for a month." Never promise bookings, only visibility and enquiries.
4. **Verified badge:** earned by identity check (phone + Instagram match) and 3 portfolio links. Cheap to deliver, valued as status.
5. **Shareable profile link + QR code:** usable in Instagram bio, WhatsApp status, business card. This is the single-player value.

### Outreach scripts

**Instagram DM (first touch, under 60 words):**
> Hi {name}! Love your {specific shot/event} from {venue}. I'm building Aperta, a directory where couples in {city} browse wedding photographers by style and budget and message them straight on WhatsApp. We're listing {city}'s best for free, and I'll build your profile myself (photos, packages, bio). Want a preview of how yours would look?

**WhatsApp (after they reply or from their business number):**
> Hi {name}, this is {founder} from Aperta. Thanks for replying. Here's a draft profile I made from your Instagram: {link}. If you like it, just send me your package prices and 12 favourite photos and I'll go live within a day. It's free for founding photographers and you keep all enquiries. Shall I proceed?

**Follow-up (day 3, once only):**
> Hi {name}, quick nudge. A few couples have already been browsing {city} wedding photographers. Happy to add you with whatever you have right now, you can fix details later.

**Voice note:** preferred over text for senior photographers; keep under 45 seconds.

Tactic: always send a **pre-built draft profile** (concierge). A finished preview converts far better than a pitch.

### Objections and answers

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| "I get enough work from Instagram/referrals" | Great, this doesn't replace that. It's one more link you share, and it shows your packages so you stop answering "price?" DMs |
| "Will you give me leads or just take my data?" | Free, you keep every enquiry; clients contact you directly on your WhatsApp. No commission |
| "WedMeGood/portals already charge me" | We don't, and we won't charge founding members for the listing |
| "I don't have time" | You send 12 photos and your prices; we do everything else |
| "Showing prices will lose me clients" | Show "starting from" and package inclusions; clients who contact you are pre-qualified. You can hide it per package |
| "Another platform is another thing to maintain" | There's nothing to log into for the first 90 days; we update it for you on WhatsApp |
| "Are my photos safe / who owns them?" | You own everything; only the images you send are shown; remove anytime |
| "How do I know you'll last?" | Fair. Listing is free, portable (you keep the link and QR), and takes ten minutes. Low risk |

### Targets (supply)

| Window | Photographers live | Notes |
|--------|--------------------|-------|
| Day 30 | 15 (with full profiles) | founder-led concierge; 10 outreach/day |
| Day 60 | 35 | referrals begin; 4-6 planners/decorators/venues joined as partners |

Funnel assumption: 100 DMs -> 30 replies -> 15 previews -> 8-10 live. Need ~500 DMs for 50 live. At 10-15 DMs/day that is about 5-6 weeks of founder time.

## 4. Client acquisition playbook

**Hook:** "Compare wedding photographers in {city} by style and budget. Message them on WhatsApp in a minute. No signup, no fees."

Never build client demand before there are 15+ strong profiles in the city.

### Channels (budget-light, ranked)

1. **Planner / decorator / venue referral partners (best ROI).** They are asked "who's a good photographer?" daily. Give them a co-branded shortlist link ("{planner}'s recommended photographers"), a QR card at venues, and free listing. Offer 10 minutes of setup. Target 10 partners by day 60.
2. **Photographers' own audiences.** Every profile has a share kit: a "Book me on Aperta" story template, WhatsApp status image, and bio link. Each photographer's followers are warm local couples.
3. **SEO pages** for "best wedding photographer in Jaipur", "wedding photographer in Jaipur under 1 lakh", by city x category x budget. Server-rendered or pre-rendered, unique copy, structured data. Compounds slowly (3-6 months **(unverified)**), cheap, and exactly how clients search. Note: the demo SPA with hash routing and no hosting is not SEO-friendly; Phase 1 needs real URLs and pre-rendering.
4. **Instagram/Reels:** "A wedding photographer under Rs 80k in Jaipur: 5 portfolios" style shortlist Reels, "Real packages explained" and "What a Rs 1.2 lakh wedding photographer actually delivers". Photographer collabs double reach. 3 Reels/week, founder-made.
5. **Google Business Profile** for Aperta plus local Maps presence; low effort, helps branded search.
6. **Couple stories / micro-influencers:** 5-10 newly-engaged local couples (Instagram 3k-30k followers) get a free "shortlist" and post their pick. Barter, not cash.
7. **WhatsApp communities / Facebook groups** for brides-to-be and city communities: contribute helpful answers (budget guides) rather than ads.
8. **Corporate (Phase 2):** HR/admin and event agencies via LinkedIn and agency partnerships; start only after wedding wedge works.
9. **Paid (only after proof):** small Instagram/Google geo-targeted tests (Rs 500-1,000/day) on couples in the wedding city, 3-6 months ahead of dates; measure cost per enquiry. Kill if above Rs 150-300 per enquiry **(arbitrary threshold)**.

### Client targets

| Window | Visits (city) | Enquiries (WhatsApp/Call/Email taps) |
|--------|---------------|--------------------------------------|
| Day 30 | soft launch, friends and photographers only; 300 | 30 |
| Day 60 | 2,000 | 200 |
| Day 90 | 6,000 | 600, spread across at least 30 photographers |

Enquiry rate target: 8-10% of visitors who view a profile tap a contact button.

## 5. Monetization (later)

| Model | Pros | Cons | Fit |
|-------|------|------|-----|
| Photographer subscription (Pro: priority placement, more images, analytics, no competitor ads) | Predictable; no need to track bookings; fits "directory only" | Needs clear enquiry volume first | **Best first** |
| Lead fee (pay per enquiry) | Aligned with value | Needs reliable enquiry tracking; photographers hate paying for "bad leads"; disputes | Second, only with in-app enquiry form |
| Commission on bookings | Highest upside | Requires booking + payments (PRD Phases 4-5); photographers and clients will go off-platform | Last; only if payments in-app |
| Featured placement / ads (planners, venues, album printers, lenses) | Simple | Needs traffic | Opportunistic |
| Client-side fees | | Kills the free hook | No |

**Switch-on triggers (all required):**
1. 50+ live photographers, with 30+ sending/receiving enquiries weekly.
2. Median founding photographer received 5+ tracked enquiries/month for 2 consecutive months, and at least 5 say unprompted they'd pay.
3. Then: launch Pro at about Rs 499-999/month or Rs 4,999/year **(price to test, not a fact)**, with the founding free tier kept free for basic listing. Test with 10 photographers first.

Do not charge before month 6. Do not charge the first 50 for the basic listing, ever (trust and referral engine).

## 8. 90-day plan (week by week)

Assumes Phase 1 hosting/admin ships in parallel; weeks start when a hosted, real-URL build exists. Week 0 is whenever that is.

**Phase 0: Prep (Weeks -2 to 0)**
- W-2: pick city; hosted build with admin and tap-counter; remove fake data; write DM and WhatsApp scripts; create Instagram handle and Google Business profile.
- W-1: build 5 pilot profiles for friendly local photographers; test on real iOS and Android devices (PRD pending item).

**Phase 1: Supply seed (Weeks 1-4)**
- W1: 10-15 DMs/day; concierge-build 5 profiles; collect feedback on profile layout.
- W2: 10 live; ask each for 3 referrals; start recording share-kit assets.
- W3: 15 live; approach first 3 planners/decorators with co-branded shortlists; first Reel.
- W4: **15 photographers live. Checkpoint 1:** if under 8, rework offer/script before continuing.

**Phase 2: Soft demand (Weeks 5-8)**
- W5: photographers share links (ask for one story + bio link each); soft launch to friends/family; weekly stats message goes out.
- W6: 25 live; 5 partners live; 3 Reels/week; first 100 enquiries tracked.
- W7: first SEO pages (top 10 city x budget queries); couple micro-influencer barter (3 couples).
- W8: 35 live. **Checkpoint 2:** need 200+ tracked enquiries and 60% of photographers with 1+ enquiry; if not, fix demand before adding supply.

**Phase 3: Proof and expand (Weeks 9-12)**
- W9: collect 5-8 photographer testimonials with numbers ("12 enquiries in a month"); case-study post.
- W10: small paid test (Rs 500/day, 7 days) if cost per enquiry from organic is known; add 2nd city decision criteria.
- W11: 45-50 live; Pro tier interviews with the 10 most active photographers; no charging.
- W12: **Checkpoint 3 (go/no-go for second city or second category):** 600 enquiries, 50 live, 80% retained, 3 organic partner referrals. Decide: expand to Phase 2 of product (reviews, enquiry form) or stay and deepen.

## 9. Top 5 risks and mitigations

1. **Supply joins, demand does not show; photographers churn.** Mitigation: do not launch demand before 15+ profiles; make link-sharing the default value; weekly stats; lead guarantee is about visibility, not bookings; kill-switch checkpoint at week 8.
2. **Photographers bypass or ignore the platform (disintermediation, low engagement).** Mitigation: free listing means nothing to bypass early; concierge updates keep content fresh; do not charge until value is proven.
3. **Portals (WedMeGood etc.) respond or out-spend us, SEO is slow.** Mitigation: stay hyper-local, free-for-photographers, style-first; lean on planners/venues and photographer audiences instead of paid search; do not compete on national scale.
4. **Seasonality and photographer bandwidth (peak season Nov-Feb, **unverified**).** Mitigation: concierge onboarding (10 minutes of their time), onboard before the rush where possible, accept slower supply during peak, and use the lean season (Jul-Oct) for the heavy onboarding push and the feature build.
5. **Trust and legal/content risk: fake seed data, stock images, photographer image rights, fake ratings.** Mitigation: remove all demo data and unlicensed images before launch (existing RELEASE BLOCKER); written consent for each photographer's images; verified badge; no ratings until real reviews exist; privacy note for enquiry data.

Honorable mentions: founder time (outreach is the bottleneck; cap at 4 hours/day), low-quality enquiries making photographers lose faith (add short enquiry form with date and budget in Phase 3), and over-promising the lead guarantee.
